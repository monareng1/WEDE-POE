## Part 2 Changelog & Iteration
* **CSS Architecture:** Refactored inline/internal styling into a unified master `style.css` stylesheet for global consistency.
* **Layout Structure:** Implemented standard `.container`, `.grid-2`, and `.home-card` structures across all pages.
* **Navigation:** Standardized links across all 6 pages to include the dedicated "Contact Us" page.
* **Gallery Enhancement:** Applied fixed-aspect-ratio containers (`object-fit: cover`) and pop-out hover effects with visible captions for all 6 gallery categories (Kitchen, Balcony, Pool, Chilling Area, Bedroom, Living Room).
* **About Page Interactive Feature:** Implemented a hover-to-expand transition on the historical overview section.
* **Amenities Page Updates:** Integrated local area highlights including proximity to O.R. Tambo Airport and East Rand Mall.
* **Contact Us Page:** Added host phone numbers (+27 82 123 4567), email placeholders, and a direct booking inquiry form.
